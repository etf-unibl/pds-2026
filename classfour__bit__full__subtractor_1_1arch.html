Detailed Description

Structural architecture of four bit full subtractor.

Structural architecture of the 4-bit full subtractor.

four bit full subtractor is made using four one_bit_full_subtractor components and carry_s signal for connecting carry in and carry out ports.

Instantiates four 1-bit full subtractors with ripple borrow.
