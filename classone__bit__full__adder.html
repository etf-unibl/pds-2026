Detailed Description

one-bit full adder

One-bit full adder.

Adds two input bits a_i and b_i together with the input carry c_i, producing the sum bit s_o and the output carry c_o.

One-bit full adder that adds a bit of the first operand, a bit of the second operand and the carry from the previous stage. The outputs are the sum of these three bits and the carry to the next stage. The circuit is purely combinational.
