Detailed Description

port of four bit full subtractor

4-bit full subtractor

Inputs are four bit numbers A, B and one bit carry in. Outputs are difference (sub_o) and carry out bit.

Subtracts two 4-bit numbers (a_i - b_i) with borrow in (c_i). Outputs the 4-bit difference (sub_o) and borrow out (c_o). Implemented in structural style using four 1-bit full subtractors.
