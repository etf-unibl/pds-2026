Detailed Description

four-bit full adder

Four-bit full adder.

Adds two four-bit numbers a_i and b_i together with the input carry c_i, producing the four-bit sum sum_o and the output carry c_o.

Four-bit full adder that adds two four-bit numbers and the input carry. The outputs are the four-bit sum and the carry to the next stage.
