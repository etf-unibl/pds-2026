Detailed Description

Structural architecture of the 4-bit comparator.

The architecture is implemented in a structural style using two instances of a 2-bit comparator. One instance compares the higher bits (3 and 2), while the other compares the lower bits (1 and 0). The final output is formed by logically combining their results.
